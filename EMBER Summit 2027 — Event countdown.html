FAQ

Good questions

Are tickets refundable?

Yes, up to 14 days before the event. After that you can transfer your pass to someone else.

Is there a student discount?

The Student pass is built for that. Bring a valid college ID at check-in.

Will sessions be recorded?

Main-stage talks are recorded and shared with every pass holder within a week.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Workshops are hands-on, so a laptop is recommended. Power strips are provided at every table.
